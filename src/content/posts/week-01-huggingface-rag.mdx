## What I learned

- **RAG is not "just call an LLM"** — chunking strategy materially changes retrieval recall.
- **Systemrounded-[50px]prompt engineering matters more than I expected** — the citationrounded-[50px]forcing prompt is the difference between a usable answer and an unverifiable one.
- **Astro 7 + Tailwind 4 changes the integration story.** `@astrojs/tailwind` only supports Astro 3–5; for Astro 7 you must use `@tailwindcss/vite` directly.
