## Problem

4 class brain tumor classification (glioma, meningioma, no_tumor, pituitary) is
a hard medical imaging problem. Classical pipelines (FFT+SVM) plateau around
82% accuracy. Quantum kernels offer a different hypothesis space — can they
beat the classical baseline on a practical medical task?

## Approach

1. **Quantum image encodings:** Implemented FRQI (Flexible Representation of
   Quantum Images) and NEQR (Novel Enhanced Quantum Representation) across
   four grid resolutions (4×4 → 32×32).

2. **QHED edge detection** + **QFT preprocessing** as quantum feature
   extractors before classical SVM classification.

3. **QSVC with ZZFeatureMap quantum kernel** as the core classifier. Compared
   on equal footing against FFT+SVM baseline.

## Result

- **84.31% accuracy at 16×16** with QSVC + ZZFeatureMap.
- **Outperformed classical FFT+SVM baseline (82.35%)** — demonstrates quantum
  advantage on a practical medical imaging task at this resolution.
- Resolution scaling analysis showed quantum advantage diminishes at 32×32
  (noise floor).
