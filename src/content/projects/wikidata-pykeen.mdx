## Problem

Knowledge graphs are everywhere — Wikidata, internal company graphs, fraud
detection networks. Given a partially observed graph, can we predict the
missing links?

## Approach

1. **SPARQL extraction:** Pulled 10k+ triples from Wikidata using SPARQL
   queries. Built a background graph from the extracted subgraph.

2. **PyKEEN graph embeddings:** Trained TransE, ComplEx, DistMult, and other
   PyKEEN methods on the link prediction task. Evaluated using MRR, Hits@K,
   and AUC.

3. **PyTorch Geometric GNN comparison:** Built a GraphSAGE-based link
   prediction model and benchmarked against PyKEEN methods.

## Result

- **Reproducible link prediction pipeline** with config-driven training.
- **MRR + Hits@K metrics** across all methods in `reports/`.
- **Streamlit explorer** for inspecting predicted links.
- **Comparison blog post** (in `/writing`) — *"Learning knowledge graph
  embeddings with PyKEEN"*.

## Why this matters

Graph/network methods are increasingly relevant to data heavy and
cybersecurity teams (fraud detection, recommendations, entity resolution).
This project is also a strong fit for any team doing recommendation or
entity resolution work.
